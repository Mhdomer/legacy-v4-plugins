NoctaCat relies on animations and runners created by talented open-source artists and developers. Every runner maintains its original license and attribution.

## Core Inspiration & Original Runners
* **Takuto Nakamura ([@Kyome22](https://github.com/Kyome22))**
  * Original creator of RunCat and the RunCat Runner Gallery ([runcat-dev/RunnerGallery](https://github.com/runcat-dev/RunnerGallery)).
  * Creator of 32 official runner animations: Classic Cat, Cat's Tail, Flash Cat, Frog, Rubber Duck, Beagle, Shiba Inu, Border Collie, Golden Retriever, Chihuahua, Dachshund, Greyhound, Jack Russell Terrier, Toy Poodle, Welsh Corgi, Dinosaur, Deer, Chicken, Bird, Dolphin, Dots, Double Crank, Escapement, Fishman, Fox, Hecken Link, Horse, Otter, Rabbit, Record Player, Squirrel, and Wankel Engine.

* **Roy C. Davies ([@reality2-roycdavies](https://github.com/reality2-roycdavies))**
  * Creator of `cosmic-runkat` smooth 10-frame pixel cat and the curled-up sleeping sprite (`sleep.png`).
